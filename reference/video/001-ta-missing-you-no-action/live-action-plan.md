# 001 Live Action Plan

Working title: TA 最近有没有想起你？他为什么没有行动？

Goal: make the video feel like a real tarot reader is sitting with the viewer, drawing cards, reading the spread, and thinking out loud.

## Visual Principle

This should not look like a slideshow.

The trust comes from:

- hands shuffling and splitting one deck;
- real cards turning over one by one;
- the reader pausing before conclusions;
- small imperfect movements: adjusting a card, touching the anchor object, breathing before a difficult sentence;
- the table staying consistent across all three groups.

The reader can be partially anonymous. Full face is optional. Hands, torso, lower face, and voice are enough.

## Camera Language

| Camera | Use | Framing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A camera | Human presence | 45-degree vertical shot; reader's hands, deck, torso, maybe lower face |
| B camera | Card proof | Overhead vertical shot; exact card layout and reversed cards visible |
| C camera | Emotional details | Macro shots of anchors, hands hovering, card corners, small pauses |

Recommended edit rhythm:

1. Start each group on the anchor.
2. Cut to hands turning cards while naming each position.
3. Hold the full six-card spread for the first reading impression.
4. Return to the reader's hands when the interpretation becomes personal.
5. Use close-ups only for the emotional center cards.

## Section Beats

### 00:00-00:50 Opening

Shot sequence:

1. Reader shuffles the deck in frame.
2. Reader cuts the deck into three piles.
3. Reader places black stone, red thread, and clear crystal beside the piles.
4. Hands leave the frame for a few seconds so the viewer can choose.
5. Minimal text: `TA 最近有没有想起你？`

Performance:

- Slow down before naming the three anchors.
- Do not sound promotional.
- Make the selection feel intuitive and quiet.

### 00:50-07:45 Group 1

Main feeling: difficult, honest, old hurt.

Key human moments:

- Pause after turning The Tower reversed.
- Let Three of Swords stay in close-up while explaining non-action.
- Lower the voice slightly for "想起是真的，但这个想起并不全是甜的".

Do not make this group visually romantic. It should feel steady and sober.

### 07:45-14:55 Group 2

Main feeling: attraction, impulse, suspended movement.

Key human moments:

- Let the red thread remain in the frame without making it theatrical.
- Slightly quicker hand movement when talking about Knight of Wands.
- Slow down again on The Moon reversed and The Hanged Man.

This group can be warmer than Group 1, but not sugary.

### 14:55-22:45 Group 3

Main feeling: clear, restrained, practical.

Key human moments:

- Place clear crystal near Queen of Swords / Four of Swords.
- Keep the card spread neat and grounded.
- Use calm, almost conversational pacing.

This group should feel like someone carefully distinguishing reality from fantasy.

### 22:45-23:20 Closing

Shot sequence:

1. All three anchors return to frame.
2. Reader's hands rest beside the deck.
3. Close on the deck or the three anchors.

Final mood:

- Not mystical.
- Not over-promising.
- Viewer leaves with agency, not dependency.

## Practical Shoot Checklist

- Shoot all three groups in one table setup so the light stays consistent.
- Record one clean audio take of the full script before editing.
- If the reader speaks on camera, do not read too fast; this script needs pauses.
- Film extra b-roll: shuffling, cutting, hands resting, anchors, full spreads, card details.
- Keep the exact drawn cards from `draw-log.md`; do not change them for prettier visuals.

## If Using AI Video

AI video should provide the human presence and table motion, not the exact card truth.

Use real card images or real filmed card inserts for exact card identities. Most video models will not reliably preserve precise Rider-Waite card faces, Chinese labels, or reversed orientation across long clips.

Best hybrid workflow:

1. Generate or shoot realistic reader/table clips.
2. Insert exact card close-ups from the real deck or `public/images/cards/`.
3. Use the real script as voiceover.
4. Add subtitles in editing, not inside the video generation prompt.

For this video, prefer the generated assets from `card-inserts/` when exact card faces are needed.
