# 001 Video Generation Prompts

Use these prompts for realistic AI video tools or image-to-video tools. They are designed to create the feeling of a real tarot reader, not a slideshow.

Important: do not rely on the video model to render exact tarot card identities. Use actual card close-ups from the deck or the site assets when the exact card matters.

For 001, the exact site-card inserts are exported by `generate-card-inserts.py` into `card-inserts/`.

## Master Visual Prompt

```text
Vertical 9:16 realistic documentary-style video of a fictional tarot reader sitting at a small table, reading tarot cards in a calm room. The reader is an ordinary real person, partially anonymous, visible through hands, torso, and occasional lower-face framing. Natural skin texture, realistic hands, no glamour filter. Dark matte table cloth, warm side lamp, one tarot deck, black stone, red thread, clear crystal. The reader shuffles, cuts the deck, turns cards one by one, pauses and interprets thoughtfully. Quiet intimate mood, grounded and reflective, no theatrical fortune-teller styling.
Camera: handheld or locked 45-degree vertical phone shot with occasional overhead close-ups of the table.
Lighting: soft warm side light, gentle shadows, realistic home-studio atmosphere.
Avoid: slideshow, static graphic cards, floating text, floating cards, magical particles, smoke, neon effects, excessive candles, fantasy costume, celebrity likeness, distorted fingers, extra fingers, unreadable hands, AI-perfect plastic skin, dramatic acting, horror mood, over-romantic music-video style.
```

## Opening Clip

Length target: 8-12 seconds per generated clip; edit several clips together.

```text
Vertical realistic phone video. A fictional tarot reader's hands shuffle one tarot deck on a dark cloth table. The reader cuts the deck into three facedown piles. Beside the piles are three simple objects: a small black stone on the left, a thin red thread in the center, a clear crystal on the right. Hands straighten the piles, then move away and leave a quiet pause for selection. Natural room lighting, warm side lamp, real table texture, calm intimate mood. No on-screen text generated by the model.
Avoid slideshow, floating graphics, magical effects, extra fingers, distorted hands, wrong number of piles, ornate fantasy styling.
```

## Group Reading Template

Use this template for each group. Generate realistic table and reader motion, then cut in exact card inserts during editing.

```text
Vertical realistic documentary-style tarot reading. A fictional tarot reader sits at a small table, mostly hands and torso visible. The reader turns six tarot cards one by one from a facedown pile, places them in two neat rows, and pauses before interpreting. The anchor object stays beside the spread: [ANCHOR]. The reader gestures gently toward individual cards while speaking, with natural hand movement and small pauses. Warm side light, dark cloth, intimate calm room, realistic phone camera footage.
No generated subtitles. No slideshow. No floating graphics. No magic effects. No glamour filter. No distorted fingers.
```

Replace `[ANCHOR]` with:

- Group 1: `a small black stone`
- Group 2: `a thin red thread`
- Group 3: `a clear crystal`

## Edit Notes

- Generate clips without text. Add Chinese subtitles later in editing.
- Use the exact card inserts from real footage or site assets when naming the card.
- For exact site-card inserts, use the generated PNGs in `card-inserts/`.
- Keep one continuous table setup so the viewer believes the reading happened in one sitting.
- Let the reader pause. The pauses are part of what makes it feel real.
- Do not create a perfect synthetic presenter. A slightly imperfect human table read is better for this format.
