# Stillpoint Tarot Pick-A-Card Prototype

Length: 104 seconds
Format: 16:9, silent pilot with bilingual on-screen captions.

## Structure

- 00:00:00-00:00:05: 未来30天，
你需要看见什么？ / What needs your attention
in the next 30 days?
- 00:00:05-00:00:13: 三组选牌 / Take a breath. Choose 1, 2, or 3.
- 00:00:13-00:00:20: 闭眼想一个问题 / Let the first quiet answer be enough.
- 00:00:20-00:00:28: 第一组：从恢复到行动 / Group 1: From Recovery To Movement
- 00:00:28-00:00:36: 你不是没准备好，是需要降低噪音。 / You are not unready. You need less noise.
- 00:00:36-00:00:44: 先把一个小承诺落地，机会会顺着行动变清楚。 / Land one small promise first; the next opening gets clearer through action.
- 00:00:44-00:00:52: 第二组：暂停是为了看清 / Group 2: Pause To See Clearly
- 00:00:52-00:01:00: 现在最重要的不是立刻决定，而是拿回判断。 / The point is not to decide instantly, but to reclaim your judgment.
- 00:01:00-00:01:08: 向一个可靠的人确认现实，再决定下一步怎么走。 / Check reality with someone steady, then choose the next step.
- 00:01:08-00:01:16: 第三组：温柔地换一个版本 / Group 3: Change Gently
- 00:01:16-00:01:24: 旧的方式正在结束，但你不需要用力证明自己。 / An old way is ending; you do not need to prove yourself by force.
- 00:01:24-00:01:32: 保留柔软，也保留边界，新的明亮会慢慢出来。 / Keep your softness and your boundaries; brightness returns gradually.
- 00:01:32-00:01:39: 把它当作提醒，不是定论。 / Use this as a mirror, not a verdict.
- 00:01:39-00:01:44: 保存你选中的组，月底回看。 / Save your group and revisit it at month-end.

## Notes

- This is an original format pilot inspired by the three-group pick-a-card mechanism, not by the reference video's visual style.
- The tone stays reflective and grounded: invitation, mirror, and self-check rather than fixed prediction.
- A voiceover version can use the same captions as a narration skeleton.
